# Ambitions & Direction

## What Excites Me Next

I'm excited about the next wave of **AI-native products** — not wrappers around GPT-4, but systems where AI is deeply integrated into the core workflow. Ask Mayday gave me a close look at what it takes to make RAG actually useful in production, and I want to go deeper on that.

I'm also drawn to **developer tooling** — products that make engineers more productive and reduce cognitive overhead. Great tools are often underrated leverage.

## The Problems I Want to Solve

- Making knowledge accessible inside organizations — Mayday is one answer, but the problem is much bigger
- Reducing the gap between what AI can do in a demo and what actually works at scale with real users
- Building products for the French and European tech ecosystem, which I believe is underserving its engineering talent

## How I Want to Work

- **Team size:** Small, high-trust teams where engineers have real ownership
- **Domain:** Product engineering close to users — not just implementing specs, but shaping what gets built and why
- **Culture:** Async-first with regular synchronous touchpoints; directness and candor over excessive process

## Availability

Open to conversations about technically ambitious roles — especially at the intersection of AI, product engineering, and developer experience. Feel free to ask me anything via the chat.
