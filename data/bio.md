# Bio

## About Me

I'm Henri Gerardin, co-founder and engineer at Mayday — a Paris-based knowledge management SaaS that helps customer service teams find the right answer, instantly. I'm currently deepening my technical expertise while helping scale a product used by 100+ companies including Fnac Darty, Doctolib, Qonto, EDF, and Free.

I care about building products that actually solve real problems, not just technically elegant solutions in search of a use case. At Mayday I've had the chance to work at the intersection of product, engineering, and growth — giving me a full-stack view of what it takes to ship software that people rely on.

## What I Care About

- **AI that works in practice** — not demos, but production-grade systems with real users
- **Developer experience** — clean APIs, good abstractions, code that new people can understand
- **Speed with quality** — shipping fast without creating tomorrow's technical debt
- **Knowledge sharing** — both in code (docs, comments, tests) and in teams

## Outside Engineering

When I'm not building, I'm following the French tech and VC ecosystem closely — particularly the explosion of AI-native startups. I'm based in Paris and occasionally co-author thoughts on the future of work and software.
